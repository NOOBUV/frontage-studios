What Frontage Studios does

Frontage Studios builds and rebuilds small-business websites so AI assistants like ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google can read, verify and cite them — then builds the wider presence that earns your business real mentions in AI answers.

Why isn't my business showing up in ChatGPT answers?

AI assistants only recommend businesses they can read and verify. If your site blocks AI crawlers, hides its content behind scripts, or lacks structured data about who you are and what you offer, assistants skip you and cite a competitor instead.
